# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to fostering an open, welcoming, respectful, and constructive community around Icebrew.

We want everyone, regardless of background, experience, identity, nationality, age, disability, personal circumstances, or level of technical expertise, to participate in the development, discussion, use, and improvement of Icebrew.

Our community should be a place where scientific curiosity, technical rigor, constructive criticism, and collaboration can thrive.

## Expected Behaviour

Community members are expected to:

- Treat others with respect, courtesy, and consideration.
- Provide constructive criticism focused on ideas, code, methods, and evidence rather than individuals.
- Respect differing viewpoints and approaches, provided they are expressed respectfully.
- Encourage participation from newcomers and individuals with varying levels of experience.
- Maintain scientific integrity, transparency, and intellectual honesty.
- Acknowledge contributions and respect intellectual property, licensing, and attribution requirements.
- Communicate professionally in issues, pull requests, discussions, documentation, and other community spaces.
- Accept reasonable feedback and corrections without hostility.
- Help maintain a community that prioritizes collaboration over hostility or personal disputes.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

The following behaviours are unacceptable:

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, or deliberate personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or conduct targeting individuals or groups.
- Insults, humiliation, or deliberately hostile responses to questions, suggestions, or contributions.
- Unwelcome sexual attention, comments, or advances.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Deliberate disruption of discussions, development activities, or community participation.
- Trolling, persistent bad-faith arguments, or abusive conduct.
- Misrepresenting research findings, contributions, technical capabilities, or project decisions deliberately.
- Any other behaviour that is reasonably deemed inappropriate in a professional, collaborative open-source community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all spaces associated with Icebrew, including:

- GitHub repositories and issues.
- Pull requests and code reviews.
- GitHub Discussions, where enabled.
- Project-related communication channels.
- Community interactions conducted on behalf of or in connection with Icebrew.

It also applies when an individual is representing Icebrew in public or other community spaces.

## Scientific and Technical Disagreement

Disagreement is an essential part of scientific and software development.

Community members are encouraged to challenge assumptions, identify bugs, question methodologies, critique implementation decisions, and propose alternatives.

However, disagreement must remain respectful and evidence-oriented.

Criticism of code, statistical methods, design decisions, scientific claims, or documentation is encouraged. Harassment, hostility, discrimination, and personal attacks are not.

Technical merit does not excuse abusive behaviour.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for enforcing this Code of Conduct.

Depending on the nature and severity of a violation, maintainers may take appropriate action, including:

- A private or public warning.
- Removal of inappropriate content.
- Temporary restriction of participation.
- Closure or rejection of issues, pull requests, or discussions.
- Temporary or permanent suspension from project spaces.

Maintainers reserve the right to take action when necessary to protect the integrity, safety, and constructive atmosphere of the community.

Enforcement decisions will be made at the discretion of the maintainers, taking into consideration the nature, severity, context, and recurrence of the behaviour.
